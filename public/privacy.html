Soundly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Soundly is a small, private, invite-only music app run by an individual for friends. This page explains what it stores about you.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use your email to recognise your account. We do not receive your password and do not request access to any other Google data (no Gmail, Drive, Contacts or Calendar).
  • App activity: songs you like, playlists you make, songs you request, and which songs you play and for how long.
  • Session data: a cookie that keeps you signed in, and the browser/device name it was created on.

How we use it

To run the app: sign you in, show your library, process song requests, and let the administrator see basic usage (for example total plays and who is active) to keep the service running within its limits.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or third parties. Data is stored with Cloudflare (hosting, database and file storage). Google provides the sign-in only.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. To have your account and activity deleted, ask the administrator who invited you, and it will be removed.

Contact

Questions about your data: contact the person who invited you to Soundly.
